Acceptable Use Policy - Information Technology Department

1. GENERAL USE
- Company IT resources are provided for business purposes.
- Limited personal use is permitted during breaks and lunch hours.
- Personal use must not interfere with job responsibilities or productivity.
- All use of company IT resources is subject to monitoring.

2. EMAIL AND COMMUNICATIONS
- Company email must be used for all business communications.
- Auto-reply must be configured when employees are out of the office.
- Large attachments (over 10MB) should be shared via approved cloud storage.
- Email encryption is required for sending confidential information.

3. INTERNET USE
- Internet access is provided for business-related research and communication.
- Access to inappropriate, offensive, or illegal websites is strictly prohibited.
- Streaming services are limited to break times and must not consume excessive bandwidth.
- Downloading unauthorized software or files from the internet is prohibited.

4. SOFTWARE USAGE
- Only licensed software may be used on company devices.
- Software licenses are managed by the IT department.
- Employees must not share software licenses or installation media.
- Open-source software requires prior approval from IT Security.

5. DATA HANDLING
- Company data must be stored on approved corporate systems only.
- Personal cloud storage services (Dropbox, Google Drive personal) are not permitted for company data.
- USB drives and external storage devices must be encrypted.
- Data must be classified according to the Data Classification Policy.

6. PASSWORD AND ACCESS
- Refer to the Security Policy for detailed password requirements.
- Access to systems is granted based on job role and need-to-know principle.
- Shared accounts are prohibited; each employee must have individual credentials.
- Access must be revoked within 24 hours of employment termination.

7. REPORTING VIOLATIONS
- Suspected violations must be reported to the IT Security team immediately.
- Reports can be made through the IT helpdesk portal or security hotline.
- Confidential reporting is available for sensitive security concerns.
- Retaliation against reporters is strictly prohibited.
