IT Security Policy - Password and Authentication

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. PASSWORD POLICY
All employees must change their passwords every 90 days.
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long.
Passwords must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and at least one special character.
Password reuse is prohibited for the last 12 passwords.
Accounts are locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts.
Single Sign-On (SSO) is required for all corporate applications.

2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
MFA is mandatory for all remote access and VPN connections.
MFA is required for accessing sensitive systems including financial and HR platforms.
Approved MFA methods include authenticator apps and hardware security keys.
SMS-based MFA is not permitted due to security vulnerabilities.
