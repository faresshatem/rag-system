Performance Management Policy - HR Department

1. PERFORMANCE REVIEW CYCLE
- Annual performance reviews are conducted every December.
- Mid-year check-ins occur in June for goal tracking.
- Probationary reviews happen at 30, 60, and 90 days for new hires.

2. GOAL SETTING
- SMART goals must be established within the first 30 days of each review cycle.
- Goals should align with departmental objectives and company strategy.
- Goals are documented in the HR self-service portal.

3. PERFORMANCE RATINGS
- Exceeds Expectations: Consistently surpasses all role requirements.
- Meets Expectations: Fulfills all role requirements satisfactorily.
- Needs Improvement: Partially meets role requirements; improvement plan required.
- Unsatisfactory: Fails to meet minimum role requirements; corrective action needed.

4. IMPROVEMENT PLANS
- Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs) are issued for ratings of "Needs Improvement" or below.
- PIPs last a minimum of 30 days and a maximum of 90 days.
- Weekly check-ins with the direct manager are required during a PIP.
- Failure to meet PIP objectives may result in termination.

5. COMPENSATION ADJUSTMENTS
- Merit increases are effective January 1 each year.
- Promotions may include a salary adjustment of 10-20%.
- Market adjustments are reviewed semi-annually for critical roles.

6. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
- Employees may appeal performance ratings through the HR department.
- Appeals must be submitted within 14 days of receiving the rating.
- A review panel of three members will adjudicate appeals within 30 days.
