Company Leave Policy - HR Department

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. ANNUAL LEAVE
Every full-time employee is entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave must be requested at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.
Unused annual leave may be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of 5 days.

2. SICK LEAVE
Employees earn 1.25 days of paid sick leave per month of employment, totaling 15 days per year.
Sick leave is accrued monthly and can be used starting from the first month of employment.
A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days.
Sick leave cannot be carried over to the next year.

3. MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
Female employees are entitled to 90 days of paid maternity leave.
Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave.
Parental leave must be requested at least 30 days before the expected due date.

4. BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the loss of an immediate family member.
For extended family members, 2 days of paid leave are granted.

5. UNPAID LEAVE
Unpaid leave may be granted at the discretion of management for personal reasons.
Requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance.
Unpaid leave exceeding 30 consecutive days may affect benefit eligibility.

6. PUBLIC HOLIDARDS
The company observes all nationally recognized public holidays.
If an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they will receive double pay or a compensatory day off.

7. LEAVE REQUEST PROCESS
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR self-service portal.
Direct managers must approve leave requests within 48 hours.
HR reserves the right to deny leave during peak business periods with 30 days notice.
