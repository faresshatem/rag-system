Employee Handbook - Human Resources

Welcome to our company. This handbook provides essential information about employment policies, benefits, and workplace expectations.

CHAPTER 1: EMPLOYMENT BASICS
- Employment contracts are issued for an initial period of 12 months, renewable automatically.
- Probation period lasts 90 days from the date of hire.
- Working hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with a 1-hour lunch break.
- Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

CHAPTER 2: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
- Salaries are paid monthly on the last business day of each month.
- Performance reviews occur annually in December.
- The company provides health insurance coverage for employees and dependents.
- A 401(k) retirement plan is available with a 4% company match.
- Commuter benefits of up to $300 per month are provided.

CHAPTER 3: WORKPLACE CONDUCT
- The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy for harassment and discrimination.
- All employees must complete annual compliance training.
- Social media policies apply to all public communications about the company.
- Confidential information must not be shared outside the organization.

CHAPTER 4: LEAVE AND TIME OFF
- Refer to the separate Leave Policy document for detailed leave entitlements.
- Employees must request leave through the HR portal.
- Extended leave of absence may be available under the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA).

CHAPTER 5: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
- The company provides an annual training budget of $2,000 per employee.
- Tuition reimbursement is available for job-related degree programs.
- Internal mobility is encouraged after 12 months of service.
- Mentoring programs are available through the HR department.
